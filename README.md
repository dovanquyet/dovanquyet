## 🔬 Research Interest

I aspire to develop AI systems that evolve through efficient self-learning and practical experience, mirroring the trajectory of human intelligence. In the verge of AGI, my research focuses on:
1. 🔍 **Long-context searching**: Advancing multi-hop reasoning in LLMs for search applications (π^2, RR).
2. 🔥 **Post-training**: Designing data synthesis pipelines to fine-tune LLMs for various skills and behaviors (π^2).
3. 🚀 **Self evolution**: Designing tasks and exploration environments that let agents naturally 1) learn to craft and apply useful tools, then inherently 2) improve its problem solving skills.
